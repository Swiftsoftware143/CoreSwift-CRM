What $1 buys, and what it does not

The Inbox plan is a door of its own, not a cut-down CRM. If all you want is a real mailbox on your own domain, this is the whole of it.

Included

  • One mailbox on your own domain, e.g. hello@yourcompany.com
  • Send and receive from it — your reputation stays yours
  • 5 GB of mail storage
  • Private inbox in the browser: read, reply, search, attachments
  • One domain to verify, one set of DNS records to add

Not included (and not charged for)

  • Contacts, companies and pipelines
  • Deals, tasks and the sales dashboard
  • Email campaigns and support tickets
  • Calendar, booking pages and automation
  • Extra mailboxes or extra domains

How it works

Your mail runs on your own mail account, which is why a mailbox can cost a dollar instead of thirty. Setup is three steps and you can stop between any of them and come back.

1

Create your free workspace

Name, email address, done — no card. Your mailbox is free for 30 days from the moment your domain verifies, not from the day you signed up.

2

Point your domain at your own mail account

You will need a domain, a Cloudflare account for its DNS, and a Mailgun account. The setup screen shows every record to paste, what each one does, and a copy button — then a single press verifies them. If a record is wrong it tells you which one.

3

Use it

Mail arrives in your private inbox within seconds. Send, reply, search, keep everything. Nothing is ever deleted for being on a free account.

Questions people actually ask

Is my mail deleted if I stay on the free plan?

No. At day 30 a free mailbox stops sending and keeps receiving. Every message you already have stays readable, and nothing is removed for being unpaid.

Do I need my own domain and mail account?

Yes, and that is the point: your mail is delivered by your own Mailgun account on your own domain, so your sending reputation is yours and a mailbox costs us almost nothing to host. The setup screen walks you through it and tells you exactly which record is wrong if verification fails.

Can I move to the full CRM later?

Yes, at any time. Your mailbox comes with you — it is the same inbox, with the rest of the CRM switched on around it — and the $1 charge stops in the same action that starts the new plan.

How many addresses do I get for $1?

One mailbox on one domain. If you need more addresses, or a second domain, the full CRM plans carry more.

Can I stop whenever I want?

Yes. Cancel and the mailbox drops back to the free behaviour — it keeps receiving, you keep your mail, and you can come back to the $1 plan later.

The Inbox plan is a standalone door: it is not a discounted CRM tier, and taking it never unlocks contacts, pipelines, campaigns or tickets.
