GET /api/dashboard/stats
Authorization: Bearer <token>

Response:
{
  "total_contacts": 42,
  "total_companies": 15,
  "total_opportunities": 28,
  "total_revenue": 5000.00
}

total_contacts, total_companies and total_opportunities are row counts for your account. total_revenue is money, not a count: it is the sum of opportunities.value over the opportunities you have marked won, and 0 when you have none. These four are the whole response — no other key is returned.

Pipeline Summary

Below the stats cards, each active pipeline shows opportunity counts per stage, giving a visual funnel of where deals are concentrated. Default pipelines are highlighted.

Global Search

Search all entities at once:

GET /api/dashboard/search/query?q=david

Matches across contacts (first name, last name, full name, email, phone), companies (name/email), and opportunities (name). Returns top 10 per type, and only from your own account.

Authorization: Bearer ***

Moving Deals Through the Pipeline

To advance an opportunity to the next stage:

  • Drag-and-drop Drag an opportunity card from one stage column to another
  • Change stage dropdown Use the stage selector on the deal card in its stage column

Both actions use:

PATCH /api/pipelines/:pipeline_id/stages/:stage_id/move/:opportunity_id
Authorization: Bearer ***

{ "stage_id": "uuid-of-next-stage" }

Value, probability, expected close date and notes are updated with PATCH /api/pipelines/:pipeline_id/opportunities/:id.

Winning & Losing Deals

When a deal reaches a Won or Lost stage, CoreSwift automatically sets the corresponding boolean flags:

  • Won: is_won = true adds value to the "Won Deal Value" dashboard stat
  • Lost: is_lost = true optionally record a lost_reason for analysis
Tip: Track lost reasons to analyze patterns and improve your sales process.

🆘 Customer Support Portal

Your Help Desk — Built In

CoreSwift CRM includes a complete customer support ticketing system. Replace Freshdesk, Zendesk, or your overloaded email inbox. Your customers submit requests. Your team manages them. Nothing leaves the platform.

No API keys. No chatbot plugins. No external subscriptions. It just works.

How Tickets Come In

Tickets can arrive through multiple channels — all tracked by source so you know where every request came from:

ChannelSource CodeHow
👤 ManualmanualYour team creates tickets in the admin Support panel
🌐 Contact FormformDrop our embed widget on any website — visitors submit tickets without logging in
📧 EmailemailForward your support@ inbox to our parser — emails become tickets automatically
🤖 ChatbotagentConnect any chatbot or AI agent via our public API — tickets created programmatically

None of these channels is privileged. A ticket created by your chatbot, your contact form, or your staff member is treated identically. No special routing, no hidden costs.

Embed Widget — One Line on Your Website

Add a floating support button to any website with one script tag:

<script src="https://coreswiftcrm.com/s/YOUR_TENANT_ID/widget.js"></script>

That gives you:

  • Floating button — bottom-right corner, matches your brand
  • Slide-out form — name, email, subject, description, priority
  • Success confirmation — visitor knows their request was received
  • No dependencies — vanilla JS, no framework, no CORS issues
  • No login required — your visitors submit tickets without accounts

Admin Support Panel

Your team manages everything from 🎫 Support in the sidebar:

  • Full queue — all tickets, filterable by status and priority
  • Create tickets — manually open tickets during phone calls
  • Reply inline — threaded messages on every ticket
  • Status workflow — open → in progress → resolved → closed
  • Priority levels — low, medium, high, urgent
  • Assignment — route tickets to team members

Customer Portal

Your end customers open 💬 My Support and see only their own requests. The portal lives at /s/<your-tenant-id>/support, and your team can open it from Support Tickets → 💬 My Support.

There is no password to issue and no email needed to sign in: the customer enters their email address plus the reference of a request they already have (the first 8 characters of the ticket id, shown on the ticket in your console). That pair proves the address, and the server hands back an access grant that never travels in a URL.

  • Submit new requests — with priority selection
  • View their history — all past tickets, statuses visible
  • Reply to open tickets — threaded conversation with your team; replying to a resolved request reopens it
  • Tenant-scoped — customers only see their own data, never another customer's and never another tenant's

This is their personal help desk — not your admin panel. They manage their own support experience.

API for Integrations

For programmatic access — connecting chatbots, Zapier, custom apps:

POST /s/:tenant_id/ticket
GET  /s/:tenant_id/widget.js
POST /api/public/contact

Set source: "agent" when creating tickets from chatbots or AI systems. The public endpoints require no authentication — designed for third-party consumption.

Pricing

Free tier: Embed widget + admin panel + customer portal included.
Paid tier: Custom domain (use your own support.yourdomain.com) + email-to-ticket parsing + priority support.

Chatbot integrations are API-only on all tiers — you connect your own chatbot, we handle the ticket routing.

13 Webhooks

Webhook Overview

CoreSwift CRM supports both incoming and outgoing webhooks for integration with satellite apps and external systems.

Satellite Apps: FunnelSwift, IncentiveSwift, WorkflowSwift, and MissedCall Respondr can push data into CoreSwift via inbound webhooks.

Inbound Webhooks (Receiving Data)

Other apps can send events to CoreSwift using authenticated webhook endpoints. The URL carries only the key prefix, which is a public index; the credential is the full key in the X-Satellite-Key header, and its SHA-256 digest must equal the digest stored for that prefix. A missing header or a wrong key is refused with 401.

POST /inbound/:key_prefix/:event_type
X-Satellite-Key: <full-api-key>
Content-Type: application/json

{ "payload": "data" }

Or the v2 endpoint:

POST /inbound/v2/:key_prefix/:event_type
X-Satellite-Key: <full-api-key>

And the v3 contact-sync endpoint — POST /inbound/v3/:key_prefix/contact-sync (also survey-complete and contact-upsert) — with the same header.

These three routes are served by the CoreSwift application itself. They are not under /api/, and the public web vhosts proxy only /api/, /s/ and /track/ to it, so on the shared host a satellite app calls CoreSwift directly rather than through the public site.

Supported event types from satellite apps:

Source AppEventAction
FunnelSwiftlead.created / lead.updatedCreates or updates a contact in CoreSwift
IncentiveSwiftentry.createdCreates contact from giveaway entry
IncentiveSwiftraffle.winnerRecords raffle winner
IncentiveSwiftloyalty.rewardRecords loyalty reward
WorkflowSwiftworkflow.completedLogs completed workflow
WorkflowSwiftinstance.advancedLogs workflow instance progression
MissedCallcall.missedCreates contact from missed call
MissedCallfollowup.createdLogs follow-up creation

Outgoing Webhook Endpoints (Sending Data)

You can register endpoints that CoreSwift will POST events to. CoreSwift delivers events to each endpoint that is active and subscribes to that event type. A workspace configures its own endpoints, signing secret and event subscriptions in the console under Settings → Webhooks: that screen lists each endpoint's name, URL, events, active state, last successful delivery and failure count, and can add, edit, pause, activate and delete them. Outbound webhooks are sold on Enterprise/Agency — a workspace whose plan lacks the webhooks module has its requests to the whole surface refused with HTTP 402 and the reason in the response body. The platform console (admin.coreswiftcrm.com) has no editor: the endpoints are workspace-scoped, so it only points here. These same calls remain available directly on the API:

POST /api/integrations/webhooks
Authorization: Bearer ***

{
  "name": "Slack Notifier",
  "url": "https://hooks.slack.com/services/xxx",
  "secret": "your-signing-secret",
  "events": ["contact.created", "opportunity.won"],
  "retry_count": 3,
  "timeout_ms": 5000
}

List configured webhooks:

GET /api/integrations/webhooks
Authorization: Bearer ***

Response: { "webhooks": [...] }

What arrives. Each delivery is a JSON POST:

POST <your url>
X-CoreSwift-Event: contact.created
X-CoreSwift-Delivery: <endpoint id>
X-CoreSwift-Delivery-Attempt: 1
X-CoreSwift-Signature: t=1759512345,v1=<hex>

{
  "event": "contact.created",
  "tenant_id": "...",
  "webhook_id": "...",
  "delivered_at": "2026-10-06T16:45:00+00:00",
  "data": { ...the event payload... }
}

Verifying the signature. When the endpoint has a signing secret the X-CoreSwift-Signature header is t=<unix seconds>,v1=<hex>, where v1 is HMAC-SHA256(secret, "<t>.<raw request body>") — compute the same value and reject a mismatch. An endpoint saved without a secret is sent unsigned.

Which events, and retries. An endpoint whose events list is empty receives every event; a non-empty list receives only those event types. An endpoint with is_active: false receives nothing. A failed delivery (anything other than a 2xx) is retried retry_count times with a growing backoff, each attempt bounded by timeout_ms; redirects are never followed. The endpoint's last_triggered_at shows its last successful delivery and failure_count how many deliveries have failed since the last success.

API Keys

API keys allow satellite apps to authenticate when sending data to CoreSwift via webhooks. Each key has:

  • Key Prefix The leading characters of the key, carried in the webhook URL to select the row. It is public and is not a secret.
  • Full Key The credential, sent in the X-Satellite-Key header. The server stores only its SHA-256 digest (key_hash), compares the two in constant time, and can never recover the full key from the row.
  • Source App Which app this key is for (funnelswift, incentiveswift, etc.)
  • Status Active or Disabled (a disabled key authenticates nothing)
  • Last Used Timestamp of most recent usage

Satellite keys have no console screen: they are provisioned per tenant by the operator, who stores the prefix and the SHA-256 digest of the full key in the satellite_api_keys table (key_prefix, key_hash, source app, active flag, last used). The keys a workspace mints itself are the Lead-delivery keys (csk_…) in Settings > Integration Center, backed by POST /api/personal-api-keys (list, rotate and revoke alongside).

🤝 Affiliate Program

Earn commissions! Refer other businesses to this service and earn a percentage of their subscription fees. The affiliate system is managed through FunnelSwift — ask your account manager to set up your affiliate account.

How It Works

    Your affiliate manager provides a unique tracking link (or you can generate one in the FunnelSwift affiliate portal).

Conversion Tracking

Attribution lives in FunnelSwift. The trigger is a plan change in this app — there is no checkout session and no payment-provider webhook anywhere in the chain:

  1. The Referral Is Recorded

    When someone arrives through your affiliate link, FunnelSwift keeps them as a lead on your affiliate account. That lead is the attribution — it is permanent, with no cookie to expire or be lost.

  2. CoreSwift Reports the Plan Change

    When the referred business moves to a paid plan here (the CoreSwift platform admin assigns the plan to the workspace — POST / PATCH /api/billing/subscription are operator-only writes, with no customer self-service plan change), CoreSwift looks up the account owner's email address and posts it to FunnelSwift's internal affiliate endpoint, POST /api/v1/internal/affiliate/upgrade-event, together with the plan name and its price.

  3. Commission Recorded

    FunnelSwift matches that email back to the lead, applies the affiliate's rate to the plan price, and records the commission against the event. A repeat of the same upgrade event is ignored, so nothing is credited twice.

This is fully automatic — nothing extra is needed as long as the customer reaches you through your affiliate link. Two details worth knowing: attribution is matched by the email address the referred business signed up with, and only a move onto a paid plan counts as an upgrade.

Affiliates are managed through the FunnelSwift Admin portal:

  • Affiliate Admin — full CRUD for affiliate products, categories, links, and conversion history
  • Affiliates — add, edit, and manage affiliate partners with commission rates
  • Affiliate Products — configure which products can be promoted and at what commission rate
  • Affiliate Links — create tracking links that pair an affiliate with a specific product

To access, log in to FunnelSwift and navigate to Super Admin → Affiliate Admin.

Private Email
