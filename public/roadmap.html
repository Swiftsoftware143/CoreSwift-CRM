Roadmap

What we are building next, what is being built right now, and what is already live in your workspace. Nothing here is a promise of a date.

Planned

On the list, not started.

  • Self-serve checkoutBuy a plan on the site instead of sending a request.
  • Folders and draftsOrganise the built-in inbox the way a mail client does.
  • More sending providersSend through a provider other than Mailgun as well as through it.
  • Team roles in depthPer-screen permissions, so a teammate sees only the tabs their job needs.

In progress

Being built right now.

  • Booking readiness, everywhereThe calendar already answers whether a customer can book it; the same clear verdict is being carried to the rest of the booking surfaces.
  • Mail setup in plain stepsAdding a domain, a signing key and a mailbox from one screen, with the guide matching what the screen shows.

Live today

In your console now.

  • Contacts and dealsContacts with CSV import and export, and a Deals pipeline with its own tab and multi-currency amounts.
  • Tickets that answerA ticket queue with replies, a response promise shown on every ticket, and a due column that tracks it.
  • AppointmentsBooking calendars with open hours and a public link, plus a readiness check that says what is missing when a customer could not book.
  • Your own mail domainSending and receiving are separate choices; a catch-all address, a 32-character signing key, and an inbox that names the customer from their own From header.
  • Email brandingUpload your logo and set a signature once; both ride every message your workspace sends.
  • Workspaces and invitationsSeparate workspaces, each with its own people and data, and invitations by email.
  • Affiliate programmeReferral links and a partner list, with the payouts you set.
  • Help centreA public help centre that never dead-ends, and a guide on your own app address.
  • Submit feedbackSend an idea, a bug or a complaint from inside the console.
Want to move something up? Vote on the full roadmap at swiftsoftware.net/coreswift-roadmap, or press Send feedback in the console and tell us which one matters to you.
