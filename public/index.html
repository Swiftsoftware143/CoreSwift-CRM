Frequently Asked Questions

How does CoreSwift CRM automate follow-ups?

Set your follow-up rules once — "when a lead enters this stage, send an SMS in 2 hours and an email in 24 hours." CoreSwift CRM handles everything automatically and logs every touch to the contact timeline. No manual reminders, no forgotten leads.

What does the AI actually do?

CoreSwift CRM's AI engine scores every lead based on behavior, engagement, and source. It identifies hot leads, routes them to the right rep via round robin, and nudges you when a deal has gone cold. It works continuously in the background — no configuration needed.

Do I really not need Calendly anymore?

If you're using Calendly for basic scheduling and you want those meetings automatically linked to your CRM contacts — CoreSwift CRM's built-in calendar does that for free. Booking pages, Google Calendar sync, automated reminders, and team round robin are all included.

How much does CoreSwift CRM cost?

The core platform is free — full pipelines, follow-up automation, calendar, SMS/email campaigns, AI scoring, event management, and cross-app sync. Premium features like private mailboxes and advanced workflows are available inside the platform when you're ready to scale, and more.

Does it work with the rest of the SwiftSoftware suite?

Natively. Contacts, tags, and events sync automatically with FunnelSwift, MissedCall Respondr, IncentiveSwift, ADASwift, and WorkflowSwift. Zero configuration — it just works.
