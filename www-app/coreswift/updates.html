What’s new

Every change below is live in your workspace. Newest first.

20 November 2026

  • A booking page says whether a customer can actually book it. If a calendar has no hours or nothing to offer, the booking screen lists what is missing instead of presenting an empty page as if it worked.
  • The receiving key is checked by its shape. An inbound signing key is 32 hexadecimal characters; anything else is refused with the reason, and a key of the wrong kind can be removed.
  • A sending key is not accepted where a receiving key belongs. The two keys look similar and do different jobs; pasting the wrong one now says so instead of failing later.
  • The inbox names the customer from their own From header. Replies are attributed to the person who wrote them, not to a routing address.
  • Stopped is not broken. A feature that needs mail stops and shows the setup it needs, so a paused mailbox reads as paused rather than as a dead button.
  • The help centre never dead-ends. Every public help page offers a way forward, and the guide on your own address is the same guide the console links.
  • Deals has its own tab, and the mailbox behaves like a mailbox. Your pipeline is one click away, and the mail pane reads like a mail client.
  • An empty key slot is metered. A credential field you have not filled in is not a key, so it no longer buys unmetered use.
  • A real call event is processed, not just acknowledged. An inbound call from the provider now runs the same path a live call does.

18 November 2026

  • Every ticket shows its response promise. The due time is on the row and in the detail, so the queue tells you what is late without opening each ticket.
  • A buyer is told how to pay, in your words. The instructions you write on the plan screen reach the pricing page a customer reads.
  • Errors speak English. Eleven panels stopped printing raw server text such as an endpoint name or “HTTP 500”, and say what failed instead.
  • The Compare Plans card promises only what a plan enforces. No feature is claimed that the plan cannot turn on.
